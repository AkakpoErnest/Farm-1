# Privacy Policy for Agribot - Smart Farming Assistant

**Last updated:** December 2024

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Personal Information
- Email address (for account creation and login)
- Name and contact information
- User role (Farmer, Customer, Expert)
- Farm-related information (farm size, crops, experience)

### 1.2 Usage Information
- App usage statistics
- Feature interactions
- Chat conversations with AI assistant
- Weather and market data preferences

### 1.3 Device Information
- Device type and operating system
- App version and crash reports
- Network connection information

## 2. How We Use Your Information

### 2.1 Core App Functionality
- Provide personalized farming advice
- Connect users with agricultural experts
- Deliver weather forecasts and market updates
- Enable community interactions

### 2.2 Service Improvement
- Analyze app usage patterns
- Fix bugs and improve performance
- Develop new features
- Ensure app security

### 2.3 Communication
- Send important app updates
- Provide customer support
- Share agricultural tips and news

## 3. Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in these cases:

- **Service Providers:** With trusted partners who help us operate the app
- **Legal Requirements:** When required by law or to protect rights
- **User Consent:** With your explicit permission

## 4. Data Security

- All data is encrypted in transit and at rest
- Firebase Authentication provides secure user management
- Regular security audits and updates
- Limited access to personal information

## 5. Data Retention

- Account data: Retained while account is active
- Usage data: Automatically deleted after 2 years
- Chat data: Deleted after 30 days
- You can request data deletion at any time

## 6. Your Rights

- **Access:** View your personal information
- **Correction:** Update inaccurate information
- **Deletion:** Request account and data removal
- **Portability:** Export your data
- **Opt-out:** Unsubscribe from communications

## 7. Children's Privacy

This app is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## 8. International Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place.
